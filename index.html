Your privacy in GYRALO

GYRALO is an offline puzzle game. The current version has no accounts, advertisements, in-app purchases, or in-app analytics services. Game progress and preferences are saved on your device.

This policy explains how Mohammed alhamad (the developer, “I”) handles information in connection with GYRALO, Android package com.gyralo.game, and support enquiries.

1. Information handled by the game

The current game does not collect or send personal information to a developer-operated server. It does not require a name, email address, telephone number, or account to play, and does not use advertising identifiers or tracking technologies.

GYRALO saves the following information locally to provide its gameplay features:

  • Your current level and unlocked levels.
  • Your best move counts and level completion progress.
  • Preferences such as sound and reduced motion.

The developer cannot remotely access these local saves. Depending on your device and backup settings, Android or your device provider may back up or restore app data under its own terms and privacy policy. GYRALO does not operate its own cloud-save service.

2. Permissions

The current game does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, or precise location, and does not ask you to upload photos or other personal files.

3. Support communications

If you email the developer, your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to provide will be received and used to respond, investigate the reported issue, and maintain the support conversation. Please avoid sending passwords, payment details, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Support correspondence is processed through the email provider and retained only as reasonably necessary to address the enquiry, maintain relevant support records, or meet applicable legal obligations.

4. Third-party services and sharing

The game is built with Unity. Unity Analytics, Unity Ads, cloud crash reporting, and engine diagnostics are disabled in the current version. No advertising or third-party analytics SDK is integrated into the current game.

Google Play, Android, and your device provider may independently process information for app distribution, security, backups, and technical diagnostics according to their own policies and your settings. Google Play may make crash, ANR, and aggregated performance reports available to the developer to help maintain the game. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

The developer does not sell personal information or share it for advertising. Support information may be processed by the email service provider or disclosed where required by applicable law.

5. Storage, deletion, and your choices

Local progress and preferences remain on your device until the app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled, subject to your device’s backup and restore features. You can manage local storage and backups through your device settings. Clearing app data may permanently remove your progress.

There is no GYRALO account to delete. If you have contacted support, you can request access to, correction of, or deletion of information held in that correspondence by emailing the address below. Requests will be handled as applicable, subject to information that must be retained for legal reasons.

6. Children’s privacy

The current game does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a support enquiry, please contact the developer so that the information can be reviewed and deleted where appropriate.

7. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled for support. No electronic storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for protecting access to your device and any device backups.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the game’s features or information practices change. The latest text and update date will be published on this page. If features such as advertisements, analytics, accounts, or cloud services are introduced, this policy and any required disclosures or choices will be updated accordingly.
